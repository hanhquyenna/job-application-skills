# DOCX CV Production Rules

These rules are mandatory whenever a Job Apply skill produces a `.docx` CV or application document.

## Required order

1. Identify the user's polished CV in the supplied application folder and treat it as the formatting reference. If there are multiple polished CVs, choose the closest department or industry lane and record the choice.
2. Read the reference structure before drafting: page count, margins, font family and sizes, heading hierarchy, spacing, section order, bullet style, date alignment, links, and any header or footer elements.
3. Read the target JD and the `experience-bank`; build the requirement-to-evidence map before writing CV text.
4. Draft the tailored content while preserving the reference's structure and visual density. Change wording and bullet selection for the JD; do not casually redesign the document.
5. Apply the reference formatting consistently across every section. Keep standard ATS-readable headings, real paragraph/list semantics, consistent dates, and stable page layout.
6. Render the DOCX to page images (and PDF when useful) and inspect every page for overflow, clipped text, blank pages, broken bullets, inconsistent spacing, orphan headings, and unreadable density.
7. Repair layout or content issues, render again, and repeat until the document is readable and complete.
8. Re-read the final DOCX and verify that every selected claim is supported by the experience bank, every required JD section is present, and no placeholder or reference-only content remains.
9. Deliver the final `.docx` and a concise match score/evidence summary. Do not deliver an unrendered draft as final.

## Preservation and truth rules

- Preserve the polished CV's visual language unless the user explicitly requests a redesign.
- Preserve factual dates, employers, metrics, and responsibility level from the experience bank.
- Never solve overflow by silently deleting a required section or weakening a supported metric; adjust wording, spacing, or bullet selection and disclose material trade-offs.
- Do not copy a different application's employer-specific claims, links, contacts, or role wording into the new CV.
- Keep the source polished CV unchanged; create a separate tailored output.
