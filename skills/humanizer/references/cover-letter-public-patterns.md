# Public cover-letter pattern ledger

This is a pattern reference for calibration. It is not a library of text to copy. Keep public examples separate from the candidate's voice and evidence.

| Source | What it demonstrates | What to avoid copying |
|---|---|---|
| [UC San Diego Career Center guide](https://career.ucsd.edu/resources/guides/cover-letter.html) | The opening names the role and contains a sentence that would fail if the company name changed; the body shows qualifications through a specific example. | Its fill-in-the-blank language is intentionally generic. |
| [University of Virginia examples](https://career.virginia.edu/resources/cover-letter-examples-templates/) | Different student backgrounds are shown through tailored examples and review by a human writing centre. | Template phrasing and the institution's sample voice. |
| [INSEAD cover-letter examples](https://www.insead.edu/system/files/2023-06/coverletter-examples-as-of-june-2023.pdf) | Finance letters state the deal, amount, method, and target contribution; another example starts with a real conversation at a recruiting event. | Prestige claims and lists of skills that turn the letter into a second CV. |
| [Santa Clara Career Center](https://www.scu.edu/careercenter/toolkit/coverletters/) | A cover letter adds the “why” and the behind-the-scenes context that a job-history document cannot show. | Broad “passion” language without a supplied reason. |
| [UC San Diego and Dartmouth guidance](https://careerdesign.dartmouth.edu/resources/cover-letter-guide/) | Employer research, concrete evidence, role-specific fit, and concise structure recur across guidance. | Generic mission statements that would fit any employer. |
| [Reddit practitioner discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/jobsearchhacks/comments/1q7byhv/people_of_reddit_how_do_you_actually_make_a_cover/) | A useful pattern is to stop summarising the CV, research a real company problem, and show how one example maps to it. | Anonymous interview claims and distinctive personal wording. |
| [Reddit recruiter discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/jobsearchhacks/comments/1d4z668/recruiters_what_do_you_ACTUALLY_want_from_a_cover/) | A letter can matter when it gives a reader a concrete detail that is absent from the CV and makes the candidate's reasoning visible. | Treating comments as representative hiring evidence. |

## Feature cards for the ING eFX Sales letter

- **Opening trigger:** a named ING FX Talking report and the decision that follows a market forecast.
- **Kien's reaction:** the number is not enough; someone still has to decide what matters to a client.
- **Primary tension:** a 64% IRR looked wrong against the 18–25% comparable range.
- **Choice:** stop and check the calculation with colleagues before it travelled further.
- **Decision stakes:** one-page reports used in internal board meetings set the maximum bid price, maximum bank lending, and the risks to take into discussions with the banks.
- **Second action:** test the bank's quarterly floating-rate risk in the debt-equity sensitivity and raise it before bank submission.
- **Supporting proof:** 10+ BIDV market-insight reports, $40M+ portfolios, 25+ client profiles, Excel and Power BI.
- **Working rule:** decide which movement deserves attention and explain why.
- **Close:** connect that working rule to a useful client conversation in ING eFX Sales.

Do not add the cause of the IRR discrepancy, the outcome of the floating-rate discussion, the audience for the BIDV reports, or a personal connection to ING unless Kien supplies it.
